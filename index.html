You're not wrong — and it's an important limitation of this data. The BLS "Sports Vehicles including Bicycles" CPI blends every bicycle type into one index: department-store bikes, kids' bikes, commuters, and $7,000 enduro machines. The index doesn't separate them — and no public data source does. High-performance bike-shop MTBs have inflated considerably faster than this blended average (see Key Findings #04). So read this dashboard as "the price of bicycles as a category" — not "the price of the bike you're riding or shopping for."

When the wage line is ABOVE a price line, that bike became more affordable. When below, less affordable.
Wages (blue dashed) grew ~91% since 2005. Entry MSRP grew 67% — wages won. Mid MSRP grew 94% — essentially tied. Premium MSRP grew 123% — prices pulled ahead, but a 2024 premium bike is categorically better than its 2005 equivalent in every dimension.
